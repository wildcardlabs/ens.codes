# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what Namepass LLC ("Namepass", "we", "us") does with information when you use ens.codes (the "Service").

## 1. What we collect

The Service has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address or payment card.

- **Wallet and transaction information.** When you connect a wallet we see its public address. Purchases, redemptions and cancellations are recorded on the Ethereum blockchain, including the wallet address, amounts and the names renewed.
- **Technical information.** Like most websites, our hosting provider logs requests, including IP address, browser type and the time of the request.
- **Redemption requests.** If you redeem a Code without a wallet, your browser sends us the details needed to submit it: the Code's signature, the names to renew and the buying wallet's address. We use the IP address of the request to limit abuse.
- **Messages.** If you contact us, we receive what you send, including your email address.

Claim codes are generated in your browser and are not sent to our servers.

## 2. Cookies and local storage

The Service does not use cookies, and it has no advertising or analytics trackers. It keeps a few settings in your browser's local storage, such as which wallet you last connected. You can clear these in your browser.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to operate the Service, to prevent abuse and keep it secure, to respond to you, and to comply with the law. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interests in running and protecting the Service, on performing our agreement with you, and on our legal obligations.

## 4. Blockchain information

Blockchain transactions are public and permanent. Anyone can see them, and neither we nor anyone else can change or delete them. A wallet address can sometimes be linked to a person. Consider this before using the Service.

## 5. Third parties

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information is disclosed only as follows:

- **Service providers** that host the site and run its server functions, under obligations to protect it.
- **Services your browser contacts directly** to make the Service work: blockchain node providers, which receive your IP address and the addresses being looked up; an ENS profile service, which receives your wallet address to return your ENS name and avatar; and the host of any avatar image set on your ENS name.
- **Your wallet provider** and any block explorer you choose to open, which have their own privacy practices.
- **Legal and safety.** Where required by law or legal process, or to protect our rights, our users or the public.
- **Business transfers.** In connection with a merger, sale or reorganisation of our business.

## 6. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Server logs are kept for a limited period by our hosting provider. Messages are kept for as long as needed to deal with them and for our records. Blockchain records are permanent and outside our control.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To make a request, contact us at legal@namepass.com. We cannot change or delete information recorded on a blockchain. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

## 8. International use

We are based in the United States, and information may be processed there and in other countries where our service providers operate. Those countries may have different data protection laws from yours.

## 9. Children

The Service is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we handle. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee security.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
